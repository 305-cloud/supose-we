About Geoff

Geoff Nelder taught Geography and IT for years before writing took over. His air-pollution research earned him a fellowship with the Royal Meteorological Society — the scientist's eye still shapes the worlds he builds. He's published around 150 short stories, and his ARIA trilogy won a Preditors & Editors Award. He's also a freelance editor, co-editor of Escape Velocity magazine, and a life member of the Vegan Society since 1975 — the root of Suppose We's vegan planet.
