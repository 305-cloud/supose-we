The Author

Robert L. Arrington

Robert L. (Bob) Arrington practices law in the Southeast, in the mountains. He is an avid reader, especially of science fiction, fantasy, historical novels, thrillers, and romance, a dedicated North Carolina Tar Heel sports fan, a devoted husband, and a “professional” uncle to his siblings’ children and their children.

He and his wife Deborah live happily with their three cats, Pyewacket, Miss Katie, and B.J. His first love has been writing since he was a teenager.

  • Day job: practicing attorney
  • Home base: the Southeastern mountains
  • Team: Carolina Tar Heels
  • Household: Deborah, Pyewacket, Miss Katie & B.J.
