A noir fantasy thriller with wings.

The Ambassador Azshael series follows Azshael through a fall, a rise, and a final reckoning — three books that trade angelic myth for something closer to noir: betrayal, consequence, and characters who bleed for what they believe.

Angel Falling opens the trilogy. Angel Rising continues it. Angel of the Final Hours brings it to a close — three acts of one long war.

A. D. Landor writes it with the instincts of someone who's told tall tales professionally for years, and the restraint of someone who knows when not to.

A. D. Landor

Author, Ambassador Azshael Series

When it became apparent that "Sorcerer to the Queen" and "Knight Errant" were no longer valid career paths, A. D. Landor worked through 37 other jobs before arriving at writer — among them looking after seals and bears at London Zoo, teaching children to ride, and writing competition scripts for radio. Landor now lives outside London, with a book and music collection that reliably keeps the neighbours from staying long for coffee.
