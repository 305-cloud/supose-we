Practical law. Impractical clients.

The Haunted Law Firm series drops real legal practice — contracts, discrimination claims, courtroom procedure — into a world where the client across the desk might be three hundred years old, or not entirely human.

A Cascade of Magic, a collection of stories, lays out how the firm came to be, as its attorneys first cross paths with ghosts, vampires, and werewolves. From there, each novel takes on its own case: a modern human-Neanderthal hybrid fighting genetic discrimination, a Christmas caseload that turns out to be anything but routine, and more.

The tone stays grounded even when the client isn't — magic with a docket number attached.

Robert Arrington

Author & practicing attorney, Southeastern United States

Robert L. (Bob) Arrington practices law in the Southeast, in the mountains. He's an avid reader of science fiction, fantasy, historical novels, thrillers, and romance, a dedicated North Carolina Tar Heel fan, a devoted husband, and a "professional" uncle to his siblings' children and their children. He and his wife Deborah live happily with their three cats, Pyewacket, Miss Katie, and B.J. Writing has been his first love since he was a teenager.
